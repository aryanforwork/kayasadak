## 1. Funnel Architecture & Conversion Strategy

The dedicated `/book-a-free-class` page serves high-intent prospects seeking a personalized trial session or structured pricing recommendation. The booking flow is designed as a **guided 4-step progressive wizard**:

- **Step 1:** Location & Service Selection (Delhi-NCR, Tricity, YTT belt)
- **Step 2:** Instructor Tier (Platinum ₹1,000, Gold ₹750, Silver ₹500) & Session Frequency (12, 16, 20 sessions/mo)
- **Step 3:** Preferred Schedule & Slot Selection (Morning 6-10 AM / Evening 5-8 PM)
- **Step 4:** Contact Details & Health Notes (Name, Phone, Instructor Gender Preference)
- **Step 5:** Instant WhatsApp Handoff (`wa.me/918273264561`)
