# Delhi-NCR Local Home Yoga Competitor Scan

## Local Competitor Profiling

### 1. Urban Company (Aggregator)
- **Model:** Platform matching independent freelancers for generic fitness/yoga at home.
- **Weakness:** High turnover, no specialized instructor tiers, lack of clinical/therapeutic focus, zero YTT backing.

### 2. Fitocube / Local Yoga Agencies
- **Model:** Local home yoga agency operating in Gurugram & South Delhi.
- **Weakness:** Opaque pricing, unverified instructor credentials, limited locality coverage outside prime hubs.

### 3. Wellinto / YogaAtHome Delhi
- **Model:** Basic directory listing independent tutors.
- **Weakness:** Unregulated quality, no standardized 3-tier qualification system, no direct WhatsApp instant booking flow.
