## H1
KayaSadhak Pricing — Platinum, Gold & Silver Home Yoga Plans

## Answer-First Statement
KayaSadhak offers transparent, tiered pricing for personalized home yoga sessions based on instructor qualifications and monthly session frequency across Delhi-NCR & Tricity.

## Complete 3×3 Pricing Table (HTML Rendered)

| Tier | Rate/Session | 3 Days/Week (12 Sessions) | 4 Days/Week (16 Sessions) | 5 Days/Week (20 Sessions) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Platinum Tier** | ₹1,000 | ₹12,000 / month | ₹16,000 / month | ₹20,000 / month |
| **Gold Tier** | ₹750 | ₹9,000 / month | ₹12,000 / month | ₹15,000 / month |
| **Silver Tier** | ₹500 | ₹6,000 / month | ₹8,000 / month | ₹10,000 / month |

---

## Qualification Criteria Per Tier (Option A)
1. **Platinum Tier (₹1,000/session):** Complete Masters + Graduation + Advanced Teacher Training Course + 10+ years experience.
2. **Gold Tier (₹750/session):** Graduation in Yoga + Advanced Teacher Training Course + 7+ years experience.
3. **Silver Tier (₹500/session):** Graduation + Diploma in Yoga + 5+ years experience.

## FAQs
- **Q:** Are there any hidden registration fees?
  - **A:** No. All prices are 100% transparent as listed above.
- **Q:** Can I switch my instructor tier mid-month?
  - **A:** Yes, you can upgrade or adjust your tier package at the start of any billing cycle.
