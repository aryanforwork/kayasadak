# Yoga Teacher Training (YTT) Content Spec

## Accrediting Body Affiliations
KayaSadhak YTT programs are offered **in tie-up/partnership with**:
1. **Indian Federation of Yoga**
2. **Yoga Alliance (USA)**
3. **AYUSH Ministry (Government of India)**

*Wording Rule:* Always state "in tie-up/partnership with". Never use "certified by AYUSH" or "registered under AYUSH".

## YTT Programs Offered
1. **200-Hour YTT:** Foundational TTC covering multi-style Hatha/Vinyasa, alignment, anatomy, and teaching methodology.
2. **300-Hour YTT:** Advanced TTC focusing on therapeutic applications, advanced pranayama, and philosophy.
3. **500-Hour Comprehensive YTT:** Complete master-level certification.
4. **200-Hour Kundalini YTT:** Specialized Kundalini awakening, Kriya, and meditation training.
5. **50-Hour Yin YTT:** Intensive restorative and fascial release training.

## Operating Footprint
Offered across 9 locations: Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, Chandigarh, Mohali, Haridwar, Dehradun, Rishikesh, Dharamshala.
