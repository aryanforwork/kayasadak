### Competitor Vulnerabilities Uncovered:
1. **Directory Dependency:** Competitors rely heavily on low-tier, automated classified directories (Justdial, Sulekha, Quickr) that carry high outbound link spam ratios.
2. **Zero Price Transparency:** Competitors conceal rates, preventing them from earning natural citations from consumer finance, personal wellness, and lifestyle publications comparing service costs.
3. **Missing YTT Destination Authority:** Delhi competitors lack authentic Rishikesh and Dharamshala ashram links, while Rishikesh ashrams lack Delhi NCR local city citations. KayaSadhak uniquely spans both.
