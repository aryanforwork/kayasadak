## Chandigarh Tricity – Combined Note
Chandigarh, Mohali, and Panchkula are commonly searched together as **"Tricity"** — consider using this term alongside individual city names in titles/meta (e.g., "Best [Service] in Tricity – Chandigarh, Mohali & Panchkula") to capture searchers who use the umbrella term as well as those who search by individual city.

## How to Use This List for Local SEO

1. **Location Pages** – Create dedicated landing pages per major locality (e.g., `/services-in-indirapuram`, `/services-in-sector-62-noida`) instead of one page per micro-area to avoid thin/duplicate content.
2. **Keyword Pattern** – Use "[Service] in [Locality], [City]" (e.g., "Home Painters in Vaishali, Ghaziabad") in titles, H1s, and meta descriptions.
3. **Google Business Profile** – Add these localities as service-area businesses if you don't have a physical office in each.
4. **Schema Markup** – Use `areaServed` in your LocalBusiness schema, listing city + top localities.
5. **Content Clusters** – Group micro-localities under their parent sector/zone (as done above) to avoid keyword cannibalization — e.g., don't create separate pages for every single Rohini sector; cluster them.
6. **NAP Consistency** – Keep Name, Address, Phone consistent across all directory listings for each city you serve.
7. **Reviews & Citations** – Prioritize getting reviews mentioning locality names naturally (customers often do this organically).
