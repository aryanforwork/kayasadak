### What a Corporate Session Covers

A typical session mixes desk-based stretches employees can do in office clothes, simple breathing techniques for immediate stress relief during a hard week, and posture-correction cues aimed specifically at long-sitting desk strain. Sessions can run 20 minutes (a quick reset, good for daily or twice-weekly slots) or a full 45–60 minutes for a weekly wellness hour. We adapt the format to whatever space you have — a meeting room, a training room, or an open floor area is enough; mats are optional for the desk format.
