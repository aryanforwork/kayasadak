### What a Typical Session Looks Like

A first session starts with a short conversation, not straight into poses: what you're hoping to get out of this, anything your doctor has told you to avoid, how your body has been feeling lately. From there it's a mix of asana, breathing work, and — depending on your goal — some guided relaxation at the end. Your teacher adjusts the sequence week to week as you improve or as your schedule changes.
