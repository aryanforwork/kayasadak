### 500-Hour Master Yoga Teacher Training in Delhi NCR

The 500-Hour Master YTT combines our 200-Hour Foundation and 300-Hour Advanced curricula into one complete program — from your very first asana instruction through clinical yoga therapeutics and deep philosophical study. It's the most direct path if you're starting from zero and know you want to train at the master level, rather than doing the 200-hour, working for a while, and coming back for the 300-hour later.

Run in Delhi NCR in tie-up with the Indian Federation of Yoga and Yoga Alliance (USA), this is the same total training depth as completing both levels separately, structured as one continuous program instead of two.

### What the Program Covers

- Everything in the 200-hour foundation: asana fundamentals, basic anatomy, pranayama, meditation, teaching methodology, and yoga philosophy
- Everything in the 300-hour advanced level: therapeutic yoga applications, advanced asana refinement, deeper pranayama and meditation, and in-depth classical philosophy
- **Sadhak-level integration:** because the program is continuous rather than split across two separate enrollments, there's more room for the foundation and advanced material to build on each other coherently, rather than having a gap of months or years between the two halves

### Who This Is For

People who are certain they want to train seriously — whether toward opening a studio, teaching full-time, or working therapeutically with students who have specific health conditions — and would rather do one complete, continuous program than split the journey into two separate courses over time. It's also a strong fit if you've been practicing for years already and want your formal training to match the depth of your personal practice from day one.
