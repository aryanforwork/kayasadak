### Our Panchkarma Therapies

- **Abhyanga (Full-Body Herbal Massage):** synchronized warm-oil massage using herbal oils suited to your constitution, aimed at circulation, muscle tension, and the general heaviness that comes from long sitting hours
- **Swedana (Herbal Steam Therapy):** herbal steam following the massage to help the body release what the oils have loosened, traditionally paired with Abhyanga rather than done alone
- **Shirodhara (Nervous System Pour):** a steady stream of warm herbal oil poured across the forehead — this is the therapy most people describe as the most immediately calming, often used specifically for stress and sleep difficulty
- **Kati & Janu Basti (Spine/Knee Care):** warm medicated oil retained over the lower back or knee using a dough well, used for localized joint and spinal discomfort

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Panchkarma safe to do at home rather than at a clinic?**
Yes, when delivered by qualified practitioners with proper equipment and authentic materials, as KayaSadhak does — the therapies themselves don't require a clinical setting, though we'll always recommend a clinic-based approach if your specific situation calls for closer medical monitoring.

**What is Panchkarma actually supposed to help with?**
Traditionally, it's used to support the body's own detoxification processes, ease accumulated stress and fatigue, and address concerns like poor digestion, joint stiffness, and sleep difficulty. It is a wellness system, not a cure for any diagnosed disease — please treat it as complementary to, not a replacement for, medical care.

**How many sessions do I need?**
This depends entirely on your constitution and goals, assessed during your first consultation. Some people book a single Shirodhara session for stress relief; a fuller detox program typically runs across several consecutive days.

**Can I do Panchkarma if I'm on regular medication?**
Tell us about any medication or diagnosed condition before booking. Many therapies are compatible with ongoing medical care, but we'll flag anything that genuinely isn't appropriate for your situation rather than proceed regardless.

**What's the difference between Abhyanga and Shirodhara?**
Abhyanga is a full-body herbal oil massage focused on circulation and muscle tension. Shirodhara is a focused therapy pouring warm oil over the forehead, generally used for stress, anxiety, and sleep concerns. They're often done together.

**Is this the same as a spa massage using "Ayurvedic" oils?**
No — we use authentic medicated herbal oils prepared for genuine Ayurvedic purposes, under Vaidya guidance, not commercially branded massage oils used in a typical spa setting.

**Can Panchkarma help with PCOD, thyroid, or back pain specifically?**
See our dedicated condition pages for how yoga specifically supports these — Panchkarma can complement that support for some constitutions, but any specific plan should come from your consultation with our Vaidya, not a generic claim on this page.
