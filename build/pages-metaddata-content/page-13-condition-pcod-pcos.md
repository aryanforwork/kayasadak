### Yoga for PCOD / PCOS & Hormonal Balance Support

A KayaSadhak teacher comes to your home in Delhi NCR to guide a yoga practice built around PCOD/PCOS — poses that support pelvic circulation and the endocrine system, breathing techniques aimed at lowering the stress hormones that are known to worsen symptoms, and a pace that respects where you are in your cycle rather than a fixed routine every session.

PCOD and PCOS involve a genuinely complex mix of hormonal, metabolic, and stress-related factors, and yoga isn't a cure for any of it — but the combination of gentle hip-opening work, stress-reduction breathwork, and consistent movement is widely used alongside medical treatment because it addresses the stress and circulation pieces directly, which medication alone doesn't always touch.

### What This Practice Focuses On

- **Pelvic circulation & hip-opening poses:** postures like Butterfly Pose and gentle reclined hip openers, aimed at improving blood flow to the pelvic region
- **Stress and cortisol reduction:** breathing techniques (including alternate-nostril breathing) specifically because chronic stress and elevated cortisol are known contributors to PCOD/PCOS symptom severity
- **Metabolic and core support:** gentle core and digestive-focused poses, relevant given the insulin-resistance component many women with PCOD/PCOS experience
- **Cycle-aware pacing:** your teacher adjusts intensity around your actual cycle rather than running an identical sequence every week

### Why a One-on-One Home Teacher Helps Here

PCOD/PCOS symptoms and severity vary enormously between individuals — irregular cycles, weight changes, mood symptoms, and fertility concerns don't show up the same way for everyone. A home teacher who knows your specific situation can genuinely tailor the practice, rather than running a generic "PCOS yoga" video sequence that assumes everyone's symptoms and priorities are identical.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Can yoga actually help regulate my cycle?**
Yoga can support the stress-reduction and circulation factors that influence hormonal balance, and many women report improvement in cycle regularity with consistent practice — but there's no guarantee, and it should be done alongside your doctor's treatment plan, not instead of it.

**Is this safe if I'm also trying to conceive?**
Gentle, appropriately modified yoga is commonly used by women managing PCOS while trying to conceive, but please discuss your specific fertility treatment plan with your doctor before adding any new practice, since some medications or procedures may call for particular caution.

**How is this different from following a "PCOS yoga" video online?**
A home teacher adjusts the practice to your actual symptoms, cycle stage, and how your body responds session to session — a generic video can't do that, and PCOD/PCOS symptoms genuinely vary a lot between individuals.

**Can this help with the weight-related symptoms of PCOD?**
The practice includes metabolic and core-focused elements that can support weight management as part of an overall approach, but yoga alone isn't a weight-loss program — see our Therapeutic Yoga for Weight Management page for a more targeted focus if that's your primary goal.

**How often should I practice for this to help?**
Most women see the practice framed as a consistent, ongoing part of managing symptoms rather than a short course — 2-3 sessions a week is a common starting point, adjustable based on how you respond.
