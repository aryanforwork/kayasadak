### Yoga Teacher Training in Rishikesh — 200 / 300 / 500-Hour Programs

Rishikesh is where a large share of the world's yoga teachers train, for a fairly simple reason: the Himalayas are right there, the Ganga runs through the middle of town, and the entire place is organized around yoga and meditation in a way no city training can fully replicate. KayaSadhak runs all five of our YTT programs here — 200-Hour, 300-Hour, 500-Hour, 200-Hour Kundalini, and 50-Hour Yin — in tie-up/partnership with the Indian Federation of Yoga and Yoga Alliance (USA).

If you've been considering a residential YTT specifically because you want distance from your regular routine while you train, Rishikesh is built for exactly that — daily life here already revolves around practice, study, and the river, so the immersion happens without much effort on your part.

### Why Train in Rishikesh Specifically

Evening Ganga Aarti at the ghats is a genuine part of the experience for most students here — not a scheduled excursion so much as something that happens most evenings a short walk from wherever you're staying. The Himalayan foothills mean early mornings are cool and quiet, which most people find makes a real difference to how a morning asana practice actually feels compared to training in a city. And because so many schools and teachers are concentrated here, you're training in a place with genuine depth of yogic tradition around you, not just inside the classroom.
