### 200-Hour Foundation Yoga Teacher Training in Delhi NCR

KayaSadhak's 200-Hour Foundation YTT is the entry point for teaching yoga professionally — it covers asana, basic anatomy, pranayama, meditation, teaching methodology, and yoga philosophy, run in Delhi NCR in tie-up with the Indian Federation of Yoga and Yoga Alliance (USA). You don't need prior teaching experience to join; most students are practitioners who want to go deeper into their own practice and come out the other side able to teach it to someone else.

This is the same 200-hour standard used across the industry globally — it's the base level almost every serious yoga teacher starts from, and the credential most studios and gyms ask for when hiring.

### What the Course Covers

- **Asana:** technique and alignment across foundational postures, with enough repetition that you can actually demonstrate and correct them, not just perform them
- **Anatomy & physiology:** how the body moves and where injuries typically come from, so you can teach safely from day one
- **Pranayama:** foundational breathing techniques and why each one is used
- **Meditation:** guided practice and an introduction to teaching it to others
- **Yoga philosophy:** grounded in classical texts (the Yoga Sutras, Hatha Yoga Pradipika, and similar sources) rather than treated as an afterthought
- **Teaching methodology:** sequencing a class, cueing verbally, hands-on adjustments, and the practical business of running or joining a studio

### Who This Is For

Anyone with a consistent personal practice who wants to teach — including complete beginners to formal training who've been practicing on their own or in classes for a while. It also suits practitioners who have no plan to teach professionally but want a genuinely deep understanding of their own practice, since the anatomy and philosophy components go well beyond what a regular class covers.
