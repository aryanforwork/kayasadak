### Yoga Teacher Training in Dharamshala — 200 / 300 / 500-Hour Programs

Dharamshala sits in the foothills of the Dhauladhar range, with McLeod Ganj — home to the Dalai Lama and a genuine center of Tibetan Buddhist culture — a short distance away. KayaSadhak runs all five of our YTT programs here, in tie-up/partnership with the Indian Federation of Yoga and Yoga Alliance (USA), for students who want their training to sit inside a meditation-heavy, culturally rich environment rather than a purely yoga-focused one.

Where Rishikesh's identity is built around the Ganga and classical Hatha lineage, Dharamshala's is shaped more by its Buddhist meditation culture and mountain quiet — if your interest in yoga leans as much toward meditation and stillness as toward asana, this setting tends to suit that emphasis particularly well.

### Why Train in Dharamshala Specifically

The altitude and mountain air genuinely change the feel of a morning practice compared to training in a plains city — cooler, quieter, and with the Dhauladhar peaks visible from most training locations in the area. The proximity to McLeod Ganj means access to a living meditation culture outside of formal class hours, which many students find deepens what they're learning inside the curriculum rather than distracting from it.

### Programs Available at This Location

All five KayaSadhak YTT programs run in Dharamshala:
- **200-Hour Foundation TTC**
- **300-Hour Advanced TTC**
- **500-Hour Master TTC**
- **200-Hour Kundalini YTT**

The curriculum for each program is identical to our other locations — what Dharamshala changes is the setting and pace, not the content or certification.

### What to Expect Practically

Training here is residential, typically in the upper Bhagsu/McLeod Ganj area, with accommodation and meals generally arranged as part of the program. Message us on WhatsApp for the specific arrangements and current batch dates for whichever program you're considering.

### Getting Here

The nearest airport is Gaggal (Kangra), roughly 20-25 km from McLeod Ganj, reachable via a domestic connection from Delhi. Overnight buses and trains to nearby Pathankot followed by a road transfer are also common, especially for students traveling from Delhi NCR who prefer not to fly.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**How is Dharamshala different from Rishikesh for YTT?**
Both offer the same KayaSadhak curriculum and certification. Rishikesh sits directly on the Ganga with a strong classical Hatha yoga tradition; Dharamshala is higher in the Himalayas with a stronger meditation and Tibetan Buddhist cultural presence nearby. Choose based on which environment you're drawn to more.

**Do I need prior meditation experience given the location's focus?**
No — the curriculum itself doesn't require prior meditation experience for any of the five programs; the setting simply offers more exposure to meditation culture outside class hours if you're interested in exploring it.

**Is accommodation included?**
Residential arrangements are typically part of the program — confirm the specifics for your chosen program and batch when you enquire.

**Can I do the 50-Hour Yin program here specifically?**
Yes, all five programs, including the 50-Hour Yin certification, run at this location.

**Is the certification the same as training in Delhi NCR or Rishikesh?**
Yes — identical curriculum, certification, and our tie-up/partnership with the Indian Federation of Yoga, Yoga Alliance (USA), and the AYUSH Ministry, regardless of which of our 9 locations you train at.

**What's the best way to reach Dharamshala from Delhi?**
Fly into Gaggal (Kangra) Airport via a Delhi connection, or take an overnight bus/train to Pathankot with a road transfer onward — we can advise further once your batch is confirmed.
