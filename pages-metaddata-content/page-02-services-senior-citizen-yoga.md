### Senior Citizen Yoga at Home — Gentle, Joint-Safe Sessions in Delhi NCR

KayaSadhak sends a verified teacher to your home to guide chair-based and gentle floor yoga built for older adults — joint mobility, balance work to reduce fall risk, and breathing practice, adapted to whatever your parent or you can comfortably do that day. No studio to travel to, no group class pace set for someone twenty years younger. Sessions run across Delhi NCR and Chandigarh Tricity, starting at ₹500 a session.

A lot of families book this for a parent who used to go to the park for a walk and has stopped — not because they don't want to move, but because the floor got harder to get down to, or a knee started aching, or they just don't feel steady anymore. Chair yoga solves the "getting down to the floor" problem directly: most of the practice happens seated or standing with chair support, so the benefit comes without the risk.

### What This Actually Helps With

Regular, gentle practice supports joint mobility in people managing arthritis, improves balance and coordination (which matters a great deal for fall prevention), and the breathing components help with sleep and general calm. None of this is a substitute for medical treatment — if there's a diagnosed condition, we ask that a doctor has cleared gentle exercise before the first session, and your teacher will always work within whatever limits you tell them about.

### What a Session Looks Like

Your teacher starts by asking about any joint pain, past surgeries, dizziness, or balance concerns, and about medications that might affect the session (some affect blood pressure or balance). From there, expect seated stretches, gentle standing poses using a chair or wall for support, and simple breathing exercises. Sessions are usually 30–45 minutes — long enough to matter, short enough not to tire.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Is yoga safe for a senior with arthritis or a knee replacement?**
Gentle, chair-based yoga is widely used for people managing arthritis and joint issues, but every case is different. We ask for your doctor's go-ahead before the first session if there's a diagnosed condition or recent surgery, and your teacher adapts every pose to what's actually comfortable.

**Does the session happen sitting in a chair the whole time?**
Mostly, yes, with some standing poses using the chair or a wall for support if your teacher judges it's safe. Nobody is asked to get down onto the floor unless they want to and can do so easily.

**How is this different from a general yoga class?**
A general class is paced for the group. Here, the entire session is built around one person's actual mobility, medical history, and comfort level that day — the pace and poses are adjusted in real time, not set in advance.

**Can my mother/father do this if they've never done yoga before?**
Yes — most senior clients are complete beginners. The first session is intentionally slow and mostly about assessing what feels comfortable.

**Can I book this for a parent and pay for it myself?**

**What if a session needs to be paused partway through?**
