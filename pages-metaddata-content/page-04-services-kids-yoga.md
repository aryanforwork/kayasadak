A KayaSadhak teacher comes to your home and works with your child one-on-one — building flexibility and body awareness for younger kids, and something closer to a focus and stress-management tool for teenagers navigating exam pressure and long school hours. Sessions run across Delhi NCR and Chandigarh Tricity, starting at ₹500.

Delhi childhood right now involves long school days, heavy bags, hours of screen time between study and downtime, and — thanks to traffic and air quality on plenty of days — less unstructured outdoor play than a generation ago got by default. A weekly home session doesn't undo all of that, but it gives a child one regular hour of guided movement and quiet breathing that isn't tied to a grade or a deadline.

### What This Looks Like by Age

For younger children (roughly 4–10), sessions lean playful — animal poses, simple games built around a posture, short guided breathing at the end framed as "quiet time" rather than meditation. For teenagers, the format shifts toward a more classical practice: proper alignment, longer holds, breathing techniques they can actually use before an exam or a stressful day, without it feeling like "kids' yoga." Tell us your child's age and temperament when you book, and we match the session style accordingly.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**What age can a child start yoga with KayaSadhak?**
We generally start from around age 4, with the format adjusted heavily for younger children. There's no upper age limit — many of our "kids" sessions are actually for teenagers managing school stress.

**Does my child need any prior experience or flexibility?**
No. Most children who start with us have never done yoga before, and flexibility develops with regular practice rather than being a prerequisite.

**Will the session actually hold my child's attention, or is it too advanced for them?**
Sessions for younger children are built around games, stories, and short segments specifically because attention span is limited at that age — a good teacher works within that rather than fighting it.

**Can this help with my teenager's exam stress specifically?**
Many parents book sessions during exam terms for exactly this reason — the breathing and focus techniques are things a teenager can use in the moment, not just during the session itself. It supports stress management; it isn't a substitute for addressing academic pressure directly if that's a bigger concern.

**How much does kids' yoga at home cost?**
₹500 to ₹1,000 per session depending on teacher tier, with monthly packages from ₹6,000 for 3 sessions a week — see the table above for the full breakdown.

**Can siblings join the same session?**
This is worth discussing when you book, since age gaps between siblings usually mean different paces are needed — sometimes a joint session works well, sometimes two shorter separate ones are better.

**Is this safe for a child with a health condition or an injury?**
Let us know any relevant medical history when booking. Your teacher will adapt the session accordingly, and for anything more serious, we'll ask for a doctor's clearance first.
