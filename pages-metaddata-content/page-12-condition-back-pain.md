### A Word on Sciatica Specifically

Sciatica pain — the sharp or shooting sensation running from the lower back down the leg — often responds well to gentle decompression and piriformis-focused stretching, but the right approach depends heavily on the underlying cause (a disc issue behaves differently than muscular tightness). This is exactly why a one-on-one home teacher who can actually see your specific pattern, rather than a generic online routine, tends to get better results — and why we ask about your specific symptoms before the first session, not after.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Can yoga actually help with chronic back pain?**
Gentle, appropriately guided yoga is widely used to support back pain management — for many people it helps with stiffness, mobility, and the muscular support that protects the lower back. It's not a guaranteed fix for every cause of back pain, and a diagnosed structural issue should always be evaluated by a doctor first.

**Is yoga safe if I have a slipped or herniated disc?**
Some poses are genuinely unsafe with an active disc issue, particularly certain forward folds and twists. We ask for a doctor's clearance and details of your specific diagnosis before beginning, and your teacher will build the session around exactly what's safe for you.

**How is this different from general poses I find online?**
An online routine can't see how your specific pain responds in real time. A home teacher watches how you move, adjusts poses immediately if something doesn't feel right, and builds the sequence around your actual pattern rather than a generic template.

**How soon will I feel a difference?**
This varies a lot by person and cause. Some people feel looser after the first couple of sessions; a genuinely stiff or long-standing issue usually needs consistent practice over several weeks to show a real shift.

**Can this help with sciatica specifically, or just general back stiffness?**
Both — the protocol above includes sciatica-specific stretches, but your teacher tailors the exact mix based on whether your main issue is sciatic nerve pain, general stiffness, or both.

**What if a pose makes the pain worse during a session?**
Tell your teacher immediately — they'll stop and adjust. No pose should be pushed through sharp or worsening pain; that's a signal to modify or skip it, not push harder.
