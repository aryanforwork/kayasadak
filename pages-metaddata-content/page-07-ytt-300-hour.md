### 300-Hour Advanced Yoga Teacher Training in Delhi NCR

The 300-Hour Advanced YTT is where a 200-hour-certified teacher goes to actually specialize — deeper asana refinement, therapeutic yoga applications, advanced pranayama and meditation, and a much more serious engagement with philosophy than the foundation course has room for. Run in Delhi NCR in tie-up with the Indian Federation of Yoga and Yoga Alliance (USA).

This isn't a repeat of the 200-hour at a harder difficulty — it assumes you already have that foundation and spends its time on what the 200-hour genuinely doesn't cover: how to actually use yoga therapeutically for common conditions, how to refine your own teaching voice and sequencing beyond a generic class template, and philosophy at a depth that changes how you think about the practice, not just what you can recite about it.

### What the Course Covers

- **Advanced asana:** alignment refinement, modifications, and safe adjustments for postures beyond the foundational level
- **Yoga therapy:** applying yoga as a tool for common physical and mental health concerns — a skill directly useful if you plan to work with students who have specific conditions, not just general fitness clients
- **Advanced pranayama & meditation:** breathwork and meditation techniques beyond the foundational set, and how to teach them safely
- **Philosophy in depth:** classical texts (Yoga Sutras, Bhagavad Gita, Hatha Yoga Pradipika) studied for genuine understanding, not surface familiarity
- **Teaching refinement:** sequencing, cueing, and hands-on adjustment skills built specifically for teachers who already have basic classroom experience

### Who This Is For

Teachers who've completed a 200-hour YTT (with KayaSadhak or elsewhere — bring your certificate) and want to specialize, whether that's toward therapeutic/clinical yoga work, running more advanced classes, or simply deepening a practice that's plateaued at the foundation level. If your 200-hour training was elsewhere, we'll ask to see the certificate and syllabus to confirm it covers comparable ground before enrolling you.

### Certification & Recognition

On completion, you receive a KayaSadhak 300-Hour Advanced certificate.
